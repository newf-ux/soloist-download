# Soloist

One task. The tools for it. Nothing else.

Soloist is a Mac menu-bar app for focus. Click the treble clef, write the one
thing you're working on, and pick the apps (and websites) you need. Everything
else is hidden. Reach for something else and Soloist asks you to stay. You can
always leave, but you have to say why.

## Download

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/newf-ux/soloist-download/releases/latest)**,
then click **Soloist.zip** under "Assets".

## Install

1. Unzip `Soloist.zip` and drag **Soloist** into your Applications folder.
2. Open it. macOS will say it "could not verify" Soloist. That's expected for a
   test version. Click **Done**.
3. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click
   **Open Anyway** next to Soloist.
4. Look for the treble clef at the top right of your screen.

Works on Apple-chip and Intel Macs running macOS 14 or later.

## Good to know

- When you allow a browser and list websites, macOS asks whether Soloist may
  control that browser. Click **OK**. That's how it blocks other sites.
- **To-do** (in the Soloist menu) needs your own Anthropic API key from
  console.anthropic.com. It's stored in your Mac's Keychain.
- Everything you do stays on your Mac.
